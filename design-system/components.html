Steps

A numbered mono sequence for how-it-works flows.

  1. 01
    Encrypt locally
    The secret is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM.
    client-side
  2. 02
    Copy the link
    Only the ciphertext goes to the server; the key stays in the URL fragment.
    key in #
  3. 03
    It self-destructs
    Opening the link deletes it from Redis with an atomic GETDEL.
    one-time
