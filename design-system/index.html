Design System

ooshare.io — Only Once Share

The design language behind ooshare.io: zero-knowledge, one-time secret sharing. A calm, deep-nav dark system built around a fixed indigo accent, a single sans face plus a mono for the cryptographic details, and motion that only ever points at what matters.

Principles

The choices that keep the product calm, legible, and trustworthy.

🌑
Deep dark. Everything lives on a near-navy canvas #0b1020. Surfaces are layered blue-greys, so depth comes from elevation steps, not heavy shadow stacks.
🎨
One accent. A fixed indigo (#6366f1) carries the brand, the gradient CTA pill, links, and focus. Status greens/ambers/reds appear only as confirmation or error.
🅰️
Two faces. Inter for everything human-readable; JetBrains Mono for the cryptographic facts — IDs, links, counters, badges — so trust-level details read distinctly.
🎞
Quiet motion. Short rises, 120–320ms, with --ease-out for releases and --ease-in-out for panels. Reduced-motion is respected everywhere.
