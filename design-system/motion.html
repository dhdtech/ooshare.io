Motion

Motion & duration

ooshare's motion is quiet and deliberate. It never decorates — it confirms, reveals, or points at state. Two easings and four durations cover the whole product, and every animation honours prefers-reduced-motion.

Principles

💡
Point, don't play. Animate only where it tells the user something: a press, a reveal, a state swap. Never animate a whole page for spectacle.
⏱
Fast by default. Most bumps are 120–260ms. The 400ms+ "entrance" flourishes other sites use are intentionally avoided here.

Easings

--ease-out for releases and presses; --ease-in-out for two-way panel transitions.

How long does a link last? ＋

Reduced motion

Every animated rule in the system is neutralised under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — no exceptions.

✓
Always respect it. The component sheet wraps .ui-btn, .ui-icon-btn, .ui-accordion-answer, .ui-nav-link, and .dropzone animations in a @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) block that disables or shortens the transition.
