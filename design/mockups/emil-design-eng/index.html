Design decisions

  • Composer-first, zero friction. The create-secret card is the hero — a visitor lands, pastes, and seals. Trust proof (how it works, security) lives below the fold where it can't get in the way.
  • Grounded in the real product. The card mirrors CreateSecret.tsx exactly: 50,000-char textarea, 10 MB dropzone, 1h–72h TTL segmented control defaulting to 24h — plus the real locale copy ("Share secrets securely", "Paste your password, API key, or private message…").
  • One page, stamped twice. Dark and light are clones of a single template, so the two themes differ only in color, never in layout. Indigo #6366f1 is the only fixed color; on light the CTA drops to indigo-600 for AA contrast.
  • Mobile-first, thumb-first. The textarea auto-focuses, the CTA sits in thumb reach above the fold, and container queries reflow the identical markup to a 920px desktop window. The CTA presses down to 0.98; reduced-motion is honored.
