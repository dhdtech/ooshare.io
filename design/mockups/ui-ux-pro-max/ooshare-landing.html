Design notes · The page IS the tool — the create-secret form is the hero, so a visitor lands and pastes; no marketing funnel in front of the action. Trust content (How it works, Security) sits below the fold for skeptical scrollers. Two states are shown, toggled in each panel bar: the form (default) and the “link created” result — both real product states. The indigo shield (#6366f1) is the only fixed color and is identical in both themes; everything else derives from calm slate/ink vs paper neutrals. Theme diff, not theme duplicates — one template is cloned into both panels, so dark and light are provably the same page and share one scroll position.
