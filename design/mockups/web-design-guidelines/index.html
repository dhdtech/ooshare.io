How it works

Three steps, then it's gone.

  1. 01

    Write

    Encrypted in your browser

    Type or paste your secret. AES-256-GCM encrypts it on your device, before a single byte is sent.

  2. 02

    Share

    Send the one-time link

    Only encrypted bytes reach us. The key travels in the link, never through our servers.

Design decisions

  • Zero friction — the paste box is the hero. No marketing wall before the tool: the textarea, file attach, TTL and the single CTA sit above the fold. A visitor can land, paste, and create in under five seconds.
  • Trust moved below the fold. The evidence badges, 3-step lifecycle and plain-language security cards still exist for skeptics — they scroll down to them instead of clicking past a landing page.
  • Mirrors the real product. Character counter, 10 MB file limit and 1h–72h TTL pills match CreateSecret exactly, so the page is the tool — honest, not an ad.
  • One accent, one action. A single indigo CTA and the fixed-#6366F1 shield are the only color; calm slate/paper, hairlines and a 4/8px rhythm hold both themes to the same disciplined register.
